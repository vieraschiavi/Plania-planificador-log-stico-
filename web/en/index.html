What it does for your business, every single day

This is not one more dashboard. Every screen ends in a concrete decision, with the reasoning in money.

Fits any ERP you run

PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, Oracle, SQLite or CSV/Excel. It auto-detects your columns even when they are named differently. No migration, no consultants.

A copilot on real data

"Which items should I discount this week?" "What do I restock today?" It answers by computing against your database and shows the table it used. It does not invent numbers.

Discounts that free up cash

It spots the stock that stopped moving and works out a fair discount, never below cost plus 8%. It tells you exactly how much tied-up cash you release.

Restock before you run out

It cross-checks real turnover against each supplier's lead time and tells you what to buy today, how much, and how much revenue is at risk if you wait.

Margins without leaks

It finds the products selling below their category margin and suggests the new price with the monthly impact already calculated.

Delivery routing

It builds the day's routes per vehicle while minimising kilometres, with or without coordinates in your data. Export the route sheet and the truck rolls.

Zones and business types

It surfaces the cross-selling you are losing: which categories similar businesses buy in other zones and yours still does not.

Everything exports

Every analysis and every copilot answer comes out as PDF, Word and Excel, ready to send to the owner, the buyer or the sales rep on the route.
