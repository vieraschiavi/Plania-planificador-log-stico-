How it works

1. You implement

Connect the client's ERP (direct database or an exported Excel/CSV), check the auto-mapping, and build the first batch of suggestions with them in the room. It is the same work you already do with other tools.

2. You get paid for the implementation

You invoice the client directly, in the USD 450–950 range depending on the size of their database. That income is 100% yours — Plania does not touch a cent of it.

3. You get paid the commission, every month

For as long as the client keeps paying their subscription, you get a percentage. You do not need to keep working there: it is paid for as long as the client stays active.
